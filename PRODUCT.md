# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

AEM practitioners, potential presenters, and people considering attending AEM Rockstar. This audience was proposed in the design interview; no narrower audience was specified.

## Product Purpose

The AEM Rockstar event site showcases practical AEM demos and the community behind them. The Rockstar 11 redesign's primary goal is building excitement and attendance.

## Operating Context

The existing English homepage provides event updates, recordings, submission guidance, topic areas, and past winners. Its content is authored in AEM Edge Delivery Services; code is maintained in this repository.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Preserve genuine content, recording destinations, and authorable content structures.
- Use the existing vanilla JavaScript/CSS AEM architecture; no framework or build step.
- Do not modify `scripts/aem.js`.
- The requested deliverable is an implemented redesign in this repository.
- Rockstar 11 event dates, registration destination, and submission-opening status are not confirmed. Do not invent them.
- Do not restore the discarded XI design plan as an approved direction.

## Brand Commitments

Keep the AEM Rockstar name and Adobe connection. The user authorized a new visual identity and requested a fun, rockstar-ish feel.

## Evidence on Hand

The live site at https://rockstar.adobeevents.com/en/ contains 2017-2026 winner portraits, a 2026 finalists recording, recap imagery, and authored event history. Treat those as historical material, not proof of an upcoming date or lineup.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Repository guidance requires responsive layouts and WCAG 2.1 AA. Preserve keyboard access, readable text, and reduced-motion preferences.
